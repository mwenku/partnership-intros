## What was weak in the first version

Email 2 used the UKG Marketplace signal, then leaked a prompt leftover: "The gain for you is Global hiring offering for existing accounts." Email 1 named Debbie and activpayroll but did not use that selected signal yet, so the first touch was generic partnership copy rather than the researched proof point.

## Which stage caused it

Writing. Discovery found Debbie Gibson as Head of Partnerships at activpayroll. Enrichment returned sources including the UKG listing, case studies, and the about page. Signal selection picked the marketplace integration. The leftover partner-gain sentence and the delayed use of that signal are writing issues.

## What changed

The rewrite dropped the leaked partner-gain line from email 2. Email 1 still introduces the Deel partnership for activpayroll's existing clients. Email 2 now carries only the UKG Marketplace proof. Emails 3 and 4 still add new sourced facts: case studies, then the 150+ country delivery claim, with an easy no.

## How the emails improved

The final sequence reads as partner recruitment for Deel, not a dumped research checklist. Follow-ups still add a new public source each time instead of "just checking in." The selected UKG signal is the personalisation hook, not a tacked-on token from the vendor brief.
